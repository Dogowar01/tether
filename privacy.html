Tether Privacy Policy

Your words stay yours.

Last updated: 8 June 2026  ·  Effective: 8 June 2026

The short version

Everything you write in Tether is stored only on your device. Nothing you type, no personal details, and no usage data is ever sent to us or any third party. We cannot read what you write — because we never receive it.

Who we are

Tether is made by Signal9 Studio, based in Australia. Questions about this policy? Email us at kirkn2025@gmail.com.

What Tether stores and where

All data created in Tether — including your name (if provided), Witness letters you choose to hold, Container entries, Anchors, and State Map history — is saved using your device's local storage only. This data:

  • Never leaves your device under normal operation
  • Is not backed up to any server operated by Signal9 Studio
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Will be deleted if you uninstall the app

If you use iCloud Backup (a device-level setting you control), Apple may include this local data in your device backup under Apple's own privacy terms, which you can review at apple.com/privacy.

Data we do not collect

Tether does not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact details
  • Your writing or journal entries
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any data for advertising or profiling purposes

Third-party services

Tether does not currently use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services. The app does not make network requests to any external server during normal use.

Children's privacy

Tether is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Because all data stays on the device and no information is transmitted, there is no server-side data to delete.

Mental health content

Tether is a supportive writing and self-reflection tool. It is not a substitute for professional mental health care, therapy, or crisis intervention.

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US) — call or text 988

Your rights

Signal9 Studio operates in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Because Tether does not transmit or store any personal information on external servers, there is no personal information held by us to access, correct, or delete.

If you believe otherwise or have a concern, contact us at kirkn2025@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy — for example, if a future version of Tether adds server-side features — we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where required, notify you within the app. Continued use of Tether after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the new terms.
